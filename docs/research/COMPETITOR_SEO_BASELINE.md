## Competitors reviewed

### LEADLIFE

- Main: https://leadlife.pro/
- DataLead: https://leadlife.pro/datalead
- WantLead: https://leadlife.pro/wantlead
- CallLead: https://leadlife.pro/calllead
- StopParsing: https://leadlife.pro/stopparsing
- Sitemap: https://leadlife.pro/sitemap.xml

Observed strengths:

- universal homepage plus separate service pages;
- tariffs, cases, reviews, articles and knowledge base;
- scalable content architecture and product-specific support sections;
- multiple proof and conversion entry points.

Risks to avoid:

- excessive overlap between homepage and product page;
- direct copying of competitor copy/claims;
- publishing many thin routes before unique content exists.

### Perehvatlidov

- https://perehvatlidov.ru/

Observed: focused long-form landing. Useful for commercial problem language, but insufficient as the structural baseline for a multi-product SEO/content platform.

### Global Soft protection

- https://global-soft.ru/#protection

Observed: protection is framed through audit and protective measures rather than an absolute guarantee. This supports a cautious promise/evidence model for `/zashchita/`.

- the brand query must be built over time rather than assumed to exist.
